Azure AI Search Basic and private connectivity make up most of the monthly cost. Azure OpenAI, Vision, Cosmos DB, Storage, and Container Apps are usage-based and inexpensive at POC traffic levels.

Workload Assumptions

  • 10,000 searches per month with one GPT-5.4 mini query-understanding call per search.
  • Average GPT call: 1,200 input tokens and 150 output tokens.
  • 80% text-vector searches; 10% image/combined searches.
  • Up to 1,000 semantic/combined searches under the configured free semantic plan.
  • One enrichment pass over 31 products; 23 products have images.
  • 1 GB Log Analytics ingestion and less than 1 GB each in Blob Storage and Cosmos DB.
  • Container Apps uses minReplicas: 0 and subscription free grants are available.
  • 730 hours per month for continuously allocated resources.

AI Usage Calculation

For 10,000 GPT-5.4 mini calls: 12 million input tokens cost $9.00 and 1.5 million output tokens cost $6.75, for $15.75/month.

The deployment capacity value of 10 is throughput quota, not reserved capacity. Global Standard is billed by token usage.

Important Notes

This is a planning estimate, not an Azure quote or invoice forecast. Public prices may differ from negotiated agreements, taxes, currency conversion, and actual measured consumption.
  • GPT image input varies with image properties; replace the blended token assumption with measured usage after representative testing.
  • Private Link data processing and egress are excluded due to low POC volume; hourly endpoint charges are included.
  • The Search shared private link is conservatively included as a third Private Link connection.
  • Container Apps free grants are subscription-wide and may already be consumed by other workloads.
  • Search Basic is continuously billed while it exists, even when idle.
  • Production sizing usually requires additional Search replicas, larger traffic assumptions, and an explicit semantic ranker plan.
